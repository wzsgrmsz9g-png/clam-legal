# Forge Strength — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 October 2026  
**Contact:** support@clam.app

## What Forge is
Forge Strength is a strength-training companion for iPhone and Apple Watch. Workouts, plans and progress are stored on your device. There is no Forge account and no Forge-operated server.

## Data Forge stores on your device
- Workout plans, set logs, drafts, equipment preferences and weekly goals
- Optional JSON backups you export yourself (unencrypted — store them privately)

## Apple services Forge may use (with your permission)
- **Apple Health:** write completed strength workouts; optionally read Forge workouts to restore set-log history
- **Apple Watch / WatchConnectivity:** sync plans and completed sessions between phone and Watch
- **iCloud Key-Value Storage (opt-in):** sync plan *templates* only — not set logs or sensor readings

Forge never uploads Health or workout data to an app-operated backend.

## Tracking and ads
Forge does not track you across apps or websites, does not show ads, and does not use advertising identifiers.

## Children’s privacy
Forge is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it to collect information from children.

## Your choices
- Deny Health access in iOS Settings at any time
- Turn off iCloud plan sync in Forge Settings
- Export or delete local data by restoring an empty backup or deleting the app (uninstall removes on-device Forge files; Apple Health records remain under your control)

## Changes
Material changes will be reflected in this document and in the App Store privacy labels.
