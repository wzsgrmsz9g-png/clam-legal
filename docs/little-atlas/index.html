Little Atlas — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026
Contact: support@clam.app

What Little Atlas is

Little Atlas is an offline family explorer for curated activities and nursing-room listings in Calgary. Catalog data and photos ship inside the app.

Location

When you tap Near me, the app uses Core Location (while in use) to sort places by distance. Coordinates are processed on device and are not sent to a Little Atlas server. There is no Little Atlas backend in this build.

Saved lists

Places you save are stored locally (UserDefaults / on-device storage).

Maps handoff

Opening directions may launch Apple Maps or Google Maps. Those apps apply their own privacy policies.

Tracking and ads

Little Atlas does not track you across apps or websites and does not show ads in this build.

Children

Family content is intended for parents/caregivers. The app is not designed to collect data from children.

Accuracy

Listings, hours and nursing-room confirmations come from sourced catalogs with review dates. They are not live availability checks. Always verify before visiting.

Contact

Use Support email above for privacy questions.
